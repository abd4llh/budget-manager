First 10 minutes

Start here

  1. Set up real accounts. Your bank, your partner's bank, joint accounts, savings and restricted accounts should each be represented once.
  2. Map connected bank feeds. A bank feed should map to the matching real account in Budget Manager, not to Outside world.
  3. Set your opening position. Reconcile accounts if you are starting from a known current balance instead of importing all history.
  4. Review synced transactions. Bank rows do not affect your ledger until you import them.
  5. Keep the monthly plan current. The active budget month is {{ active_month|date:"F Y" }}.

The most important distinction

Budget month vs bank date

A transaction always keeps its real bank date. Separately, each budget impact has a budget month. In salary/pay-cycle mode, your salary opens the following budget month.

ExampleSalary booked 28 August → September budget opens.Groceries booked 28 August → counted in September.The bank date still remains 28 August.

If a transaction truly belongs to another month, you can manually override its budget date when editing the transaction.

Where is the money?

Accounts

Accounts describe where money physically sits, not what it is for.

Available
Checking/current accounts and everyday cash.
Savings
Money you still own but intentionally keep aside.
Restricted
Money you own but cannot freely spend yet, such as a blocked account.
External / bookkeeping
Where money enters or leaves the tracked system. These accounts are normally excluded from net worth.

Ownership is separate from purpose. An account can now have one or several household owners. Select both people for a joint account; linked household logins remain separate from the ownership metadata.

What is the money for?

Budget categories

Categories classify the budget effect of a transaction:

Funding
Money available to a budget cycle, such as Employment (Net) or a blocked-account release.
Expense
Money consumed by the household budget, such as groceries, rent or a transfer you intentionally treat as spending.
Savings
Money deliberately allocated to a savings purpose.

You can add new categories directly from the monthly Budget page. Each monthly budget line also has its own optional note, useful for listing that month’s subscriptions, a planned gift, a one-off payment, or other context without changing the category globally.

What happened?

Transactions

A transaction has two layers:

  1. Account movement — where money moved from and to.
  2. Budget impact — whether that movement is Funding, Expense, Savings, or no budget impact at all.
Example: groceriesMain account → Outside world, €40Budget impact → Expense / Groceries, €40
Example: moving money to savingsMain account → Joint Savings, €500Optional budget impact → Savings / Emergency fund, €500Net worth does not change because you still own the money.

Automatic imports

Bank sync

Bank connections are read-only. Budget Manager stores the authorised session and imported bank observations; it does not store your bank password and it cannot initiate payments.

Background refreshes are automatically spaced at least six hours apart per bank connection to respect common Open Banking unattended-access limits. You do not need to press Sync now as part of normal use.

Sync now is for when you are actively using the app and want a fresh result immediately. It sends the browser context required for an interactive/PSU-present request. A bank can still apply other temporary limits, so repeated manual refreshing is unnecessary.

A bank feed must be mapped to the corresponding real Budget Manager account before its rows can be imported correctly.

Nothing enters the ledger silently

Review inbox

Synced bank transactions first arrive in the Review inbox. From there you can:

  • filter by words, bank, date, amount and direction;
  • import a row with the correct budget category;
  • use Import + pair reimbursement for an incoming repayment from a shared purchase;
  • pair two bank rows as an internal transfer;
  • ignore rows you do not want in the ledger;
  • select several pending rows and ignore them together.

Ignored is reversible. Use the Ignored status filter to restore a row later.

Avoid fake income and spending

Internal transfers

If €500 leaves one account you own and €500 arrives in another, that is one internal transfer. It should normally have no income or expense impact.

The inbox suggests possible pairs when dates, currencies and opposite amounts look like the two sides of the same transfer.

Shared purchases

Reimbursements without bookkeeping gymnastics

When you pay for yourself and someone else, import the purchase normally. When their repayment appears in Review, use Import + pair reimbursement, then choose the original purchase. You can also pair any already-imported incoming payment from its transaction page.

ExampleOriginal purchase: €100 Expense.Friend reimburses: €40.Pair the €40 incoming transaction with the €100 purchase → Budget Manager changes the effective purchase expense to €60 and removes any income classification from the repayment.

Version 2.7 intentionally supports one reimbursement pair per purchase to keep this workflow simple and reversible. If you need several people reimbursing one purchase later, that can be expanded.

Make balances trustworthy

Opening balances & reconciliation

Opening balances establish where an account started. Reconciliation compares the app's calculated balance with a real bank statement balance on a specific date.

If you import historical bank transactions, do not also set a current opening balance that already includes those same transactions, or you will count the money twice.

Predictable money

Recurring items

You may not need recurring rules at all if Bank Sync already brings the real transaction into Review. Recurring is mainly useful when you want Budget Manager itself to create a predictable transaction.

  1. Movement: choose where money leaves and arrives, just like a normal transaction.
  2. Schedule: set frequency, start date and next run.
  3. Budget impact: choose Funding, Expense or Savings only when that movement should affect the monthly budget.
  4. Posting mode: start with manual. Auto-post creates ledger entries automatically, so do not use it for the same item if you also import the bank transaction or you may duplicate it.
Example: fixed rent without Bank SyncMain account → Outside world, €448.37 monthly.Budget impact → Expense / Rent.Auto-post only if the amount/date are stable and you are not importing the same payment elsewhere.

Debt tracking

Loans

The Loan page does not create a loan by itself. It adds APR/payment information around a real Loan account.

  1. Go to Accounts and create an account with type Loan. Enter the amount currently owed as the opening balance.
  2. Go to Loans → Add Loan profile and select that account.
  3. Enter lender, original principal, APR, regular payment and payment day.
  4. Record/import actual payments as transactions. As the Loan account balance changes, the payoff estimate changes too.

The payoff calculation is an estimate based on the account balance, APR and regular payment; it does not replace your lender statement.

What the numbers mean

Dashboard & reports

The Dashboard opens on the active budget month, not necessarily the calendar month. Reports use budget months for Funding, Expenses and Savings, while net worth is based on actual account movements and balances. Period presets (3M, 6M, 12M, YTD) set the range directly; editing From/To switches the report to a Custom range.

Work faster

Search, filters & calculator

The main data pages use the same advanced-filter pattern as the Review inbox. Start with the common filters, then open More filters for date, amount, ownership, status and page-size controls where relevant. Search accepts multiple words and narrows results to rows matching those words.

The Review page is available directly in the top navigation because bank identification is a normal daily workflow, not a Bank Sync setup task.

A calculator is available from every page using the floating Calculator button. It is non-modal: you can drag or minimize it and continue editing the page underneath while it stays open. It supports parentheses and the standard +, −, ×, ÷ and % operators, and you can type directly with the keyboard.
