Pay-cycle budgeting

Your bank transaction keeps its real date. In Salary / pay-cycle month mode, a salary transaction assigned to the selected funding category opens the following budget month.

28 Aug · Salary→ September budget opens28 Aug–27 Sep · spending→ SeptemberNext salary arrives→ October opens

If salary arrives earlier or later because of a weekend or holiday, the actual salary date becomes the boundary automatically. A manually entered Budget date remains an override.
