Recurring is optional when bank sync is your source of truth

Use a recurring rule when you want Budget Manager to create predictable ledger transactions for you. If salary, rent or subscriptions already arrive reliably through Bank Sync, you can simply classify them from Review instead and avoid duplicate entries.
