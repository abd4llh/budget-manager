Bank connection privacy notice

This is a private, self-hosted household budgeting application. When a bank connection is authorized, account information is requested through the configured Open Banking provider and stored on the household's own Budget Manager server.

Data used

The application may store bank name, masked account identifiers, account balances, booked transaction data, authorization/session identifiers, and the mapping to Budget Manager accounts. It does not ask for or store the user's online-banking password.

Purpose

Bank information is used only to stage transactions for personal budgeting, reconciliation, and household financial reporting. Payment initiation is not implemented.

Control

A connection can be disconnected from Budget Manager. Previously imported ledger transactions are retained unless the household user deletes them separately.
