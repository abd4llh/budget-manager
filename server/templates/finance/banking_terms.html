Bank connection terms

The bank-sync feature is intended solely for private, non-commercial household use. It provides read-only account-information synchronization into Budget Manager and does not initiate payments.

The account holder must authorize access directly with the bank/Open Banking provider and may revoke that authorization. Availability, history depth, consent duration, and transaction detail depend on the connected bank and provider.

Budget Manager remains the household's accounting record. Synced bank data is staged for review before it is posted to the ledger; bank balances are shown as observations and do not silently rewrite ledger balances.
